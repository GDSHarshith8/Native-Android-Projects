# LiterTrack

### Your Reading Journey, Uninterrupted.
Meet **Literatrack**, the simplest way to track your reading journey. It's a lightweight, offline-first app designed for one thing: helping you read more. No distractions, no clutter—just your books.

We believe reading should feel rewarding, not like a chore. That's why Literatrack puts you in control, giving you a beautiful, organized space to celebrate every finished page and discover your next great read.

---

### **Effortless Book Tracking**

Literatrack makes managing your reading list a seamless experience.

* **Build Your Wishlist:** Found a book you want to read? A quick search is all it takes to add it to your list for later.
* **Track Your Progress:** With a simple swipe, move books from "Wishlist" to "Reading" and then to "Completed," celebrating every finished book.
* **Stay Organized:** A quick long-press lets you instantly remove a book, while fluid gestures make editing your list effortless.

---

### **Built for Simplicity**

Every part of Literatrack is designed to feel natural and intuitive, so you can focus on the books that matter.

* **Fully Offline:** Your reading list is always with you, no internet needed. All your data is stored locally on your device, so you're never dependent on a signal or a login.
* **Minimalist Design:** The clean, distraction-free interface lets you enjoy smooth animations and fluid gestures that make navigation feel effortless.

---

### **Powered by the Google Books API**

Literatrack taps into a vast database to make finding and adding books a breeze. You can:

* Search for books by title, author, or ISBN.
* View detailed information, including descriptions and cover images.
* Seamlessly add accurate data to your list without manual entry.

---

### **Under the Hood**

Literatrack is built with modern Android technologies to ensure a fast, beautiful, and reliable experience.

-   **Kotlin:** The core programming language for a concise and safe codebase.
   
-   **Jetpack Compose:** Powers a declarative and performant UI.
   
-   **Material 3:** Delivers a beautiful and consistent design with the latest Android standards.

-   **Coroutines:** Handles asynchronous operations efficiently and without blocking the UI.
 
-   **Hilt:** A dependency injection framework that reduces boilerplate and makes code more testable.
 
-   **Preferences DataStore:** A modern and safe way to store lightweight user data.

-   **Room Database:** A robust database library for local data persistence.

-   **Google Books API:** Fetches rich book details like title, author, description, and cover images to enhance your reading list.
